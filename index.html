ABOUT US

Welcome to Our School

A place where every child is valued, encouraged and inspired.

Growing Together, Learning Together

Kolavalloor West LP School is committed to providing a joyful, inclusive and child-centred learning environment.

We believe every child is unique and has the potential to become a confident, creative and responsible citizen.

Our school combines academic learning with arts, sports, cultural experiences, technology, environmental awareness and life skills.

  • Child-centred education
  • Activity-based learning
  • Creative opportunities
  • Values and life skills
  • School-community partnership
OUR JOURNEY

School History

A legacy of learning, service and community.

Kolavalloor West LP School

Our school has been an important part of the local community, nurturing generations of children through education, values and meaningful learning experiences.

Over the years, the school has grown through the dedication of teachers, students, parents, alumni and well-wishers. Our journey reflects a commitment to education and the development of our community.
